Packs and Camps

From the Ashen Path: Dark Blade wiki
Packs and Camps
Contents
  1. 1 The alarm
  2. 2 The attack queue
  3. 3 Greed is punished
  4. 4 How to pull a pack apart
  5. 5 On the approaches

Enemies in Ashen Path live in camps. They come in groups; don't count on fighting them one at a time. But a group follows rules too, and you can use those rules.

The alarm

  • The first enemy to notice you shouts. Within 0.1–0.33 s its whole camp joins in.
  • Any awake regular enemy within 7 m that can see you also joins.
  • Those who join call only their own camp; the chain reaction goes no further.
  • A shout does not wake sleepers. But the Wailer and the Drowned Bell-Ringer wake everyone within 9 m, sleepers included.
  • Elites don't answer shouts and don't call anyone themselves (except their own camp).
  • An enemy killed by a silent Stealth and Backstab does not shout.
  • In some places camps share an alarm: the bell in the yard of The Last Lodging, the horns in Thornwood and on the keep walls. Set one off and several rise.

The attack queue

The game limits how many regular enemies wind up or strike at the same time:

Two regular enemies never start attacks less than 0.2 s apart. Ranged enemies don't shoot while another blow is flying at you, and shoot only when they themselves are visible on screen.

Greed is punished

When you start a swing within an enemy's reach, it may answer at once. The chance: knight, duelist, king 40%; the Bell Matron and the Rootmother 30%; the Warden 25%; brute, shieldbearer, pikeman, torchbearer 22%; everyone else 12%. Elites have a 1.3 times higher chance.

How to pull a pack apart

  1. Start with the shooters and the screamers. The Wailer wakes everyone around with her wail, crossbowmen hold the ledge, the Torchbearer leaves burning ground behind. While they live, the fight only grows.
  2. Fall back to a narrow spot. A doorway, a walkway, a stair: the enemies line up and come one at a time.
  3. Lead them far away. An enemy "loses" you if you are more than 16 m away, or out of sight and more than 6 m away. After 4 s it gives up, but no sooner than 2 s after someone from its pack last saw you. Then the whole pack goes home and heals to full. On the way back they will notice you again if you are closer than 70% of their sight range.
  4. Pull them out one by one. A camp wakes as a whole, but enemies from other camps standing to the side do not. Approach from the edge so that only one camp sees you, not two.
  5. Quietly pick off the edges. A backstab kills a regular enemy at once and without a shout.
Careful

Inside a keeper's arena, enemies never leave and never give up.

On the approaches

On Normal and Ashen there are "approaches under fire": crossbowmen hold a ledge and shoot every 1.6–1.8 s from up to 13.5 m. Move from cover to cover and count the shots. On Story, ranged enemies don't hold the ledge.
