The patient will not die. I promised.

keeper title card

Master Veil, the Queen's Physician, is the keeper of The Grey Lazaret. He fights in the anatomical theatre while hollow students sit on the tiers.

Special trait: he does not die

Veil cannot be killed in the real world.

  1. When his health drops to zero, he gets up with 45%: "The patient will not die. I promised."
  2. This happens twice.
  3. On the third fall the fire turns black and the first visit to the Alter World begins. There Veil returns with 60% health, and he can only be killed with Rage.

That is why Veil's base health is halved (×0.5): per "life" it is 880 / 2503 / 2628 on the three difficulties.

Tactics

The in-game tip: "His scalpel strikes in threes — the third is late." This is true: the pause between the second and third blow of the series is longer than between the first and second. Do not rush the third parry.

  • "He hears you" is a homing charge. It comes more often if you are far away or he has just jumped back. Like the Warden's charges, it breaks against a pillar.
  • The saw and the amputation are wide sweeps: roll inward, behind him.
  • On Story Veil is slower than the running hero (2.5 m/s against 4) and rests longer. On Normal his speed is capped at 2.9 m/s. On Story and Normal a second full hit within 1.25 s of the first gives you 0.75 s of invulnerability — protection against being "clicked to death".
Note

The in-game tip says: "In the grey, stand still: he hunts by sound." The fight code has no separate hunting-by-sound mechanic: "He hears you" is an ordinary homing charge.

Reward

An Ember Shard, the "Rage" strike for good, and the keepsake Unlabelled Vial — "The remedy he never gave the queen. Inside: ash."

Spoiler: who he is

He is the one who told King Aurel: "Until spring." And a hundred years ago he took a sick girl from her father and promised to keep her until spring. His journal: "Spring never came. I keep her." In detail — The King, the Queen and the Physician.
