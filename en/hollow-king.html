At last… Take it from me. If you can.

keeper title card

The Hollow King, "Aurel, Who Could Not Let Go", is the last keeper, at the top of The Pale Throne. The arena is the "Throne of Ash", ringed by braziers. This is the longest fight in the game: on Normal the King has 18,050 health.

The King's rest between moves is the shortest in the game (0.23–0.73 s).

The braziers

The in-game tip: "When the Flame leaves the armour, the armour barely feels your blade. Snuff the burning brazier instead."

  1. At the change to the second phase the Flame flies, in 0.8 s, into the burning brazier farthest from you.
  2. While the Flame is out of the armour, the King takes only 30% damage, moves 30% slower and rests longer.
  3. Every 4 seconds a "Pale lash" flares under you: a 1.25 m circle, an explosion after 0.9 s, 100 damage, unblockable. On Ashen the circle is not shown.
  4. Strike the burning brazier: it has about two of your blows' worth of health. A snuffed brazier goes out for good, and the Flame jumps to the next farthest one. Unlit braziers only ring.
  5. After the third brazier is snuffed, the Flame returns and the third phase begins. At this change the King loses 12% of his maximum health and is open for 2.5 s.

If instead you beat him down to 30% by striking the armour, the third phase begins with no health loss and no window.

Tactics

  • The grasp (190 damage) cannot be parried or blocked: the hand glows like an ember in the windup — roll at once.
  • Vanishing cut: the King vanishes and reappears 1.7 m behind you 0.1 s before the blow. The blow can be parried — turn around.
  • Ash nova is the heaviest blow in the game: a 3.5 m circle around him, a windup of exactly one second. Get out early.

Reward

The Ashen Greatsword and the keepsake The Hollow Crown — "Light as ash. Inside the rim, one name: AUREL. Room has been left for another." After the fight comes the choice at the throne.

Spoiler: who he is

The King who could not let anyone go. He lit the Flame to keep his dying wife. The Flame burned him hollow from within and now wears his armour. Under the helmet in the third phase there is not emptiness. In detail — The King, the Queen and the Physician and Endings.
