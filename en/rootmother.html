keeper title card

The Rootmother, "Liane, Queen of the Orchard", is the keeper of Thornwood. She sits on a throne of roots in the Heart of the Wood.

The crushing coil punishes you for standing right up against her. And she picks projectiles and bursts less often if you are within 3.2 m of her edge. Choose a middle distance.

Heart-roots

The in-game tip: "While the heart-roots glow she heals. Cut them — three blows each — and she breaks."

  • Roots grow at the change to the second phase (three) and to the third (two). They appear at the points farthest from you: 7 m to the left and right of the throne and at the entrance.
  • A root has about three of your blows' worth of health.
  • Each root older than a second heals her by 0.18% of her maximum per second. She will not heal above the upper limit of her current phase.
  • Cut the last one, and Liane is open for 2.5 s.
  • Roots can grow again no sooner than 25 seconds later, through the "Drink the ash" move.

Reward

The Feather Knot ring and two Ember Shards. Keepsake Queen's Ring — "Found around a root. Inside, worn almost away: “A. to L. — stay.”"

Spoiler: who she is

Queen Liane, Aurel's wife. The Grey came for her in her twenty-fourth spring, and that same night the King lit the Flame. She would not be kept as ash and walked into the orchard barefoot. The roots drank the ash, and in her wood the dead at last stayed down. If Elsa brought her comb, Liane will have one more last line. In detail — The King, the Queen and the Physician.
