The Grey Lazaret

It smells of soap and ash here. The soap won.

The Grey Lazaret is the fourth world. A hospital for the Grey Sickness on a cliff: quarantine walls, grey sheets on lines, an apothecary garden, an anatomical theatre, the queen's rooms. Keeper — Master Veil. Here, too, comes the first visit to the Alter World. This world ends the first act.

Map

One map — The Grey Lazaret (map). 3 shrines (Quarantine Gate, Queen's Rooms, Garden Door), 2 secrets, no shortcuts or locked doors. An ambush in the Stretcher Yard; the elite is the Plague Doctor in the Apothecary Garden.

Characters

  • Beata the Nurse — in the Common Wards. She needs the ward journal.
  • Elsa, Lady-in-Waiting — in the Queen's Rooms. She asks you to carry a comb.
  • Maelis — at the Quarantine Gate, but she does not go inside.

Enemies

Ashen Husk, Village Pikeman, Rot Witch, Drowned Arbalest, plus the Shrouded Sick, Orderlies and the Plague Doctor.

What to watch for

  • In the Common Wards there are six shrouded sick. Their coughing leaves clouds in the air.
  • Orderlies strike with stretchers in a straight line — step off the line.
  • An ambush in the Stretcher Yard, plus an arbalest.
  • Master Veil does not die in the real world. Prepare for the Alter World and for the choice it forces on you.
Spoiler: the world's story

On the Cliff Stair the hero's feet start counting on their own. This is a playable memory: a hundred years ago a father carries his sick daughter up forty steps to the lazaret, and a physician in a beaked mask says: “Until spring.” At the top waits that same physician. After the fight: an empty cot with the card “INA” and small bare footprints going down the stairs. At the fire the hero says it for the first time: he had a daughter. In detail — The Hero and Ida.
