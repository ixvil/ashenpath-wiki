keepers' title card

The Keep Champions are Ser Hal and Ser Bryn, "the Twin Oaths", keepers of Ember Keep. They step out of the forge fire in Champion Hall. Hal is a heavy knight with a shield; Bryn is light, with two blades.

How they fight together

  • One attacks, the other guards behind the shield and circles to flank you, about 4.2 m from you on the opposite side. Bryn starts, Hal guards.
  • The guarding one strikes only with ranged moves: Hal with a shield charge, Bryn with a thrown dagger.
  • They never strike at the same time: the second waits until the first's windup and blow are done, plus another 0.13 s.
  • Every 9 seconds (10 on Story) they swap places. If the attacker is broken or stunned, they swap at once. The new attacker comes in with an oath leap (a red blow) if farther than 2.8 m away.

When one falls

The survivor kneels over the body (2.85 s, invulnerable) and then fights alone — this is the second phase, "For Two". Alone, the survivor is about a third faster.

  • Hal alone gains only the double "Sister's cut".
  • Bryn alone takes her brother's shield (shield 140), raises it for 0.83 s after taking two hits and gains the shield rush.

The in-game tip: "Strike the one that attacks; the guarding one waits to flank you. Fell Bryn first: Hal alone is slower."

Counter stance is a blue glow lasting 0.77 s. Hit her during it and you take a 110 counter that cannot be blocked. The stance has no ring. Do not strike: wait.

Tactics

  • Hit the attacker. Blows against the guarding one go into the shield: 8% of the damage gets through, and light blows bounce off.
  • A broken attacker is swapped out at once — use the window immediately.
  • Bryn first: Hal alone is slower and has fewer moves.

The last blow

When the last of the two falls, he drops to one knee. The finishing blow is a separate press, and the game waits for it.

Reward

As elites, Hal drops the Halberd and Bryn the Blood Oath ring. Keepsake — Twin Oath Ribbon: "A knot of red ribbon tied around two wrists. It held better than the wrists did."

Spoiler: who they are

The children of Gorm and Greta. Their father took them to the Keep, "not the pit". This fight is the heaviest point of the story. In detail — Gorm, Greta, Hal and Bryn.
