Pale Flame

From the Ashen Path: Dark Blade wiki
Pale Flame

Under his throne he lit a pale fire that burns death itself.

prologue

The Pale Flame burns beneath the Pale Throne. Since it was lit, "nothing here truly dies. It burns, and the ash walks on." The Flame gives light but no warmth: the throne hall is cold.

What is known about it

  • King Aurel lit it the night the physician said of the queen: "Until spring."
  • Its roots run beneath the whole mountain. In the Coal Mines you can see that the Flame is deeper than the throne: ember shards are its cooled "bones", and the Keep's forge feeds them to its blades.
  • In the fight with the Hollow King the Flame leaves the armour and hides in the braziers.
Spoiler: what it lives on

The Flame has eaten the king hollow and wears his armour. It needs a new "wick" — someone to sit on the throne. The mural on the last climb: the throne is a candle, the king its wick, and the pilgrims below wait their turn. How its story ends is up to you: Endings.
