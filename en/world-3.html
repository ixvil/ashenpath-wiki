Drowned Chapel

Water to the knees. Somewhere above, a bell tolls for no one.

Drowned Chapel is the third world. The Chapel of the Tolling rang for every soul that left. When souls stopped leaving, Mother Isolde opened the sluices: ash drowns, and the drowned stay down. Water to the knees, the nave to the waist. Keeper — The Bell Matron.

Total: 4 shrines, 7 secrets, 2 shortcuts, 1 locked door, 2 ambushes, 9 fragments.

Characters

  • Sister Wren — at the entrance, in the Bell Gallery, in the belfry. Choice: give her the Bell Clapper.
  • Brother Anselm — in the Scriptorium. He is looking for the page without names.
  • Mira, the Drowned Novice — in the Novices' Cells.
  • Pell the Pilgrim — in the nave Under the Choir and at the entrance.
  • Maelis — on the way to the chapel.

Enemies

Censer Monk, Drowned Arbalest, Chapel Shieldbearer, Ashen Husk, plus Drowned Choristers and the Drowned Bell-Ringer.

What to watch for

  • Under the Choir: three arbalests above waist-deep water. Move from pillar to pillar.
  • The Bell-Ringer and the choristers raise the alarm.
  • Bells on the maps can be struck: they stagger enemies within 5 m, cooldown 5 s.
  • Ambushes on the Organ Stair and in the Flooded Nave.
  • The well by the bell tower is a drop: a fall costs 12% of health.
Spoiler: the world's story

In the nave Ida silently takes down an arbalest on the choir loft, and a fresh chalk ladder appears on a pillar. Wren rang “for the swallows”: one swallow kept circling over the lazaret's children's wing. Isolde tells the hero: “You are already dead.” He answers her — his first line in a fight: “I'm here.” At the fire in a cell the hero and Ida admit that both remember their death on the road. In detail — The Story of Ashen Path.
