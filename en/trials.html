Trials

From the Ashen Path: Dark Blade wiki

Trials are a separate mode of arenas with no story.

TrialArena's world
Hold the Pyreworld 1
Bell BridgeDrowned Chapel
Thorn MazeThornwood
UnseenThornwood
Twin OathsEmber Keep
Ballista GauntletEmber Keep
Lantern DarkThe Pale Throne
Keepers' EchoThe Pale Throne

There is also a daily trial and a boss rush against every keeper in a row. The boss rush opens only after you finish the whole journey.

Before buying the full game, Hold the Pyre and the daily trial are available. The rest comes after the purchase (see Full Game and Purchase). No essence drops in trials.
