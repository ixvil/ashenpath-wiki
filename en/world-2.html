Hands on the milestone. Thousands of hands. The stone is worn into a bowl.

The Grey Crossing is the second world. A muddy road along a grey river, wayside shrines, the Last Lodging inn, a toll bridge with a tower, laundries, a fishing village and a ferry in the mist. Here you see where the pilgrims the Warden sent home really went. The warmest world in the game. Keeper — Tobias the Ferryman.

The start of the world

Right past the gate of the first world, a story scene begins on the road to the toll gate. In it you briefly control Ida (see Playing as Ida). This scene is part of what you can play before purchase; after it you need the full game.

Total: 4 shrines, 7 secrets, 2 shortcuts, 1 locked door, 3 ambushes and arenas, 12 fragments.

Characters

  • Marta the Innkeeper — in the Common Room of the Last Lodging. Errand “The son's lantern”.
  • Lucius the Collector — at the Toll Gate. He will take a coin.
  • Pell the Pilgrim — by the hearth in the Common Room.
  • Ida — by the well, on the Yard Roof, on the ferry.
  • Maelis — at the Ferry Landing.

Enemies

Ashen Husk, Cinder Cur, Village Pikeman, Drowned Arbalest, plus Wandering Pilgrims (they walk in threes) and the Hookman.

What to watch for

  • The bell in the Inn Yard links two camps: raise one and both come (six enemies).
  • Toll Bridge: two arbalests on the tower and a Hookman who drags you in with his hook. Cover: an overturned cart.
  • Drowned Landing: an ambush of three.
  • Smokehouse: a closed arena in smoke; everyone's vision drops.
  • Water is everywhere: a roll in water is shorter.
Spoiler: the world's story

On the road Ida walks ahead and does not look back. At the toll gate a portcullis drops: the hero is locked in, and Ida fights the headsman Morten. The fight cannot be won: Morten forces her to her knees by the portcullis, and she crumbles to ash. Morten's men knife the hero in the back. This is “Ash on the Road”.

Both rise again. Ida now has a chalk-white streak in her hair. At the Last Lodging Ida weaves her blue ribbon into the hero's cloak. On the bridge she saves him for the first time. Tobias recognises her: as a little girl she rode his ferry without a coin. In detail — The Story of Ashen Path.
