"The Keep on Fire" is the third visit to the Alter World, in Ember Keep. The main choice of the game is made here.

When

After Champion Hall, by the fire on the wall. The fire does not turn black — it goes out completely. The ash hangs in the air.

Part 1. The race

The Keep is burning from inside. Up the ballista stair, toward the forges, a small light creeps — an ember pulled along by a fiery thread. There is no timer bar. There is only its light: over 90 seconds it fades to 5% (it never goes out completely).

  • Flasks don't work. Each blow heals 3%.
  • The burning knights don't hold formation — they come in a crowd: up to four at once (on Story and Normal up to two, less often and weaker).
  • Halfway there, two figures stand back to back. The only way on is through them.
  • The goal on screen: "Goal: run to the Great Forge — follow the fiery thread". The game's hint: "don't stand and fight, break through".
  • The race ends at the Great Forge.

Part 2. The choice

The Double stands at the anvil. Cupped against his chest is that same ember. He holds out the hero's own sword to him, hilt first: "Take it. At last you've become yourself." He promises: "I'll bring her back. We have fire enough."

Two signs with no words: a sword and an open hand. The choice cannot be undone in this journey.
