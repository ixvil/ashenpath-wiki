The Bell Matron, Mother Isolde, is the keeper of the Drowned Chapel. The great bell of the Chapel of the Tolling still hangs over the flooded nave.

Answering the toll

This is the core mechanic of the fight. The in-game tip: "The great bell's toll rings gold: tap GUARD as it touches you and the whole nave staggers, her too."

  • The Great toll comes from the bell by the back wall: a short hum, then a ring spreads 13 m in 1.4 s.
  • If your Guard window (parry or block) is open at the moment of contact, the toll is answered. The direction you face does not matter.
  • An answer counts as a perfect parry: +30 Ember. Isolde is open for 2.17 s, and every drowned one in the arena dies.
  • If you do not answer, the wave cannot be blocked: only rolled.
  • On Ashen the window is only 0.12 s and there is no ring — only the sound.

The drowned

In the second phase two hollows rise at the edges of the arena: they have 55% of a normal hollow's health and 60% of its damage, and give no essence. They wait until Isolde attacks. They come back only when both are dead and 25 seconds have passed. An answered toll kills them at once.

Tactics

  • The red bell slams hit a 1.8 m circle right in front of her: move sideways, not back.
  • The safest window is after the three-slam chain (1 s) and after answering the toll.
  • In the third phase the pendulum strikes along a line in the direction she faces. Do not stand straight in front of her at range.

Reward

The Band of Breath ring and an Ember Shard. Keepsake Bell Clapper: "Without it the dead forget, slowly, that they are dead." See Keepers' Keepsakes.

Spoiler: who she is

She rang for ten thousand dead. When the dead stopped leaving, she opened the sluices with her own hands, and the river came into the chapel. The rope was held by a seven-year-old girl — Wren. After the fight Wren will ask for the bell clapper.
