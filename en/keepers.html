Keepers

A keeper is a world's boss. In each of the ten worlds the keeper stands at the end of the last map, beyond the fog. Every keeper has three phases except the Champions: that pair has two.

How health works

A keeper's health = base × difficulty multiplier × world curve × the author's adjustment. The author added the adjustment on 27 October: Veil's health was halved, and every keeper after him was raised in steps. The Rootmother got +15%, Gorm +30%, the Champions +45%, the Nameless +60%, Morten +75%. The Hollow King got ×1.9 and then ×2 again, for ×3.8 in total.

If you change difficulty mid-fight, the share of remaining health is kept.

Damage

Move damage in these articles is the base value. In a fight it is multiplied by the keeper's multiplier for your difficulty and reduced by your defence. The "fixed share" rule of Ashen does not apply to keepers.

Phases

When a blow takes health below a threshold, it stops at the threshold: the extra damage is lost, and you cannot skip a phase. The keeper begins to transform. While that lasts (about 1.85–1.95 s), he is invulnerable and harmless. From the second phase on he moves 12% faster.

Shared rules

  • A parry removes a third of the keeper's poise (a perfect parry, half). If his poise breaks, the keeper is open for 1.83 s. Otherwise he staggers for 0.43 s (0.73 s after a perfect parry).
  • Recoil. After a parry the keeper does not start a new attack for 1 s, after a block for 0.6 s (10% less on Ashen).
  • Whiff. If a heavy keeper move with a recovery of 0.5 s or more misses, he is "open" for the whole recovery: damage and poise damage ×1.5.
  • Windup armour. During the windup and active frames, hits to his poise are half as strong.
  • Charges into a pillar. Five moves (the Warden's charges, Tobias's ram, Veil's "He hears you", Gorm's "Out of the dark") break against a pillar or wall: the keeper is open for 2.17 s.
  • A keeper never leaves the arena. Enemies inside the arena do not go home.
  • Any melee blow can be blocked except two: the Warden's grab "Sent back down" and the Hollow King's "Grasp". The ringing waves of the toll and the traps of the Rootmother and the King cannot be blocked either.
  • Greed punishment. If you start a windup next to a resting keeper, there is a 28% chance (40% for the Champions) that he breaks off his rest and strikes at once.

Difficulty cues

  • On Story every keeper windup is 15% longer (active frames and recovery do not change).
  • On Ashen there are no rings, flashes, ground circles or markers. What remains: the pose, the windup sounds (light, heavy, a "delayed" low hum for windups of 0.73 s or more, a shot), the coloured weapon trail during active frames (gold, red, white) and a short "ting" at the start of the press window.
