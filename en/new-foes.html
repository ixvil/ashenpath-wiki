Version 2.0 added variant enemies. Each is built on a base type (its habits and health) and got its own name, look and signature move.

World 1

Roofer

Base: Drowned Arbalest. A rooftop sniper: lobs tiles in an arc, with a longer windup than usual; you can roll out of the throw. Found on the Rooftops, Crooked Street and the Mill Stair.

Torchbearer

Base: Ashen Husk. The torch sweep (70) sets the ground on fire for 3 seconds. Do not stand in the fire. Found in the Market Square, the Burning House and The Feast Meadow.

Wailer

Base: Ashen Husk. The wail (30) knocks you back and cannot be parried. The main thing: the first time she spots you, she wakes every enemy within 9 m, even sleeping ones. Kill her first, ideally with a strike in the back. Found in Ambush Alley and in the flooded Lower Town.

Shovel Apprentice

Base: Village Pikeman, health ×1.35. A wide 180° sweep of the baker's peel (112), then a slow turn. The game's advice: roll inside the sweep and strike his back. Market Square.

World 2

Wandering Pilgrim

Base: Village Pikeman. Staff sweep and staff jab. They walk in threes. Pilgrims' Hollow, the Inn Yard, the Road to the Bridge, the Laundries.

Hookman

Base: Village Pikeman. The hook pulls you toward him and cannot be parried. The game's advice: roll when the hook glints. Toll Bridge.

World 3

Drowned Chorister

Base: Censer Monk. A cone-shaped scream reaching 4.2 m. Organ Gallery.

Drowned Bell-Ringer

Base: Ashen Husk. His hand-bell wakes the nave: like the Wailer, the first time he spots you, he wakes everyone within 9 m. Bell-Ringers' Yard.

World 4

Shrouded Sick

Base: Ashen Husk. His cough leaves a grey cloud. Six in the Common Wards, two in the Steam Laundry.

Orderly

Base: Village Pikeman. A stretcher ram in a straight line (100). Step off the line. Queen's Rooms and the Stretcher Yard.

Plague Doctor

Base: Rot Witch, elite. Throws a flask and hops back. Apothecary Garden; drops an Ember Shard.

World 5

Bee Swarm

Base: Cinder Cur. Stings through a guard, but drowns in water: lead it into a bog or a pond. The Queen's Apiary.

Thorn Spitter

Base: Drowned Arbalest. Shoots from the slopes. Thorn Slope and the Apple Terraces.

Sleeping Soldier

Base: Bramble Brute. Sleeps in the "Sleeping Soldiers" camp. The two camps are linked: if one wakes, so does the other.

World 6

Hewer

Base: Bramble Brute. A pick strike from the wall (120), cannot be parried. Sleeps in the Drifts.

Keep Overseer

Base: Village Pikeman. The whip pulls you in. The game's advice: roll toward him. The Drifts.

World 8

The Unnamed

Base: Ember Duelist. Fights with a weapon like yours. Three on the field, one on the Ledges.

World 9

Palace Guard

Base: Chapel Shieldbearer. Thrusts with a halberd and holds the line in threes: break one shield and the line falls apart. The Palace Gate, The Parade Stair.

Chained Prisoner

Base: Ashen Husk. A lunge on the chain. The Prison Tower.

Courtier

Base: Ember Duelist. Rapier lunge. The Ministries.
