Go back. I will not ask again.

keeper title card

The Ashen Warden is the Keeper of the First Gate and the first boss of Ashfall Village. On his health bar his name is Oswin. He waits on a stone bench in the Warden's Yard, among the packs, staves and boots of the pilgrims he sent home.

How to prepare

  • Rest at the Well Square shrine: there are no more shrines until you win.
  • Any weapon will do. The starting Broadsword is a good choice: it blocks well and ripostes fast.
  • Soul level does not matter. What matters is learning two things: what to parry and where to roll.

The ember sweep leaves three burning patches in front of the Warden. They burn every 0.33 s, cannot be blocked and do not depend on difficulty.

The key trick against the Warden: the pillar

The in-game tip: "His charge never stops. Wait in front of a pillar and roll aside: he breaks on the stone." If the charging thrust or the shoulder rush slams into a pillar or wall, the Warden is open for 2.17 seconds — riposte. He picks the charge more often if you are far away (more than 6 m) or if he has just jumped back from you. And if you hug him for a long time, he leaps back, and for the next 1.5 s a charge is five times as likely.

Phase tips

  • The Wall. Parry the gate sweep and the shove. "Pilgrim's End" is an overhead blow along a straight strip: step aside or take it on your block, but do not parry it. Stay near the pillars.
  • Forty Winters. The ember sweep can be parried even though its ring is white. After the sweep, do not stand in the burning patches. The leaping slam (red) — roll at the last moment, not earlier. The "Vow" series ends with a red cleave: after the two gold cuts, be ready to roll.
  • Go Back. The grab cannot be blocked or parried — only rolled. The Warden's palms glow like embers as it comes. On Story and Normal the grab cannot kill the hero if he had more than 25% health before it: his health just drops to 1. On Ashen it kills.

A shout from the roof

If you talked to Ida on the roofs or at the well, in the third phase, below 15% health, she shouts from the gatehouse roof. The Warden stops dead for 1.5 s and is open. Once per fight.

Reward

  • The Iron Seal ring and an Ember Shard.
  • Keepsake: Pilgrim's Token — "A bronze token stamped with a stair, worn smooth by a thumb." See Keepers' Keepsakes.
  • After the fight the "Warden's Rest" shrine lights up in the yard.
Spoiler: who he is

Oswin is a pilgrim Maelis woke long before the hero. He was the only one who reached the throne — and came back down. For forty winters he stood at the first gate and turned pilgrims back in a way that would not kill them. Once a barefoot girl came to him, and on the fortieth time he let her stay. His greatsword stays planted in the yard, and you can take it under a condition from the second visit to the Alter World. In detail — Oswin's Story.
