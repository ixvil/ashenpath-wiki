Come here. Head — straight.

keeper title card

Morten the Headsman, "The Last Sentence", is the keeper of Aurel's Palace. He fights in the Headsman's Yard among the blocks. You meet him much earlier than the ninth world: he steps onto the road at the start of the second world.

Tactics

The in-game tip: "Roll the grab, never block it. When he lays his head on the block, strike — the window is short."

  • The grab is red and cannot be parried. A roll is the safest answer. (In the code it lacks the "unblockable" flag, so a block does hold it, but it costs a lot of stamina.)
  • After "Axe on the block" he has his longest recovery — 1.17 s. That is the short window to strike.
  • "Sentence" is a long strip straight in front of him. Step aside.
Note

The fight description says the executed rise and strike along the chains, and that between blows he lays his own head on the block. The fight code has no separate enemies: the "chains" are lines of bursts. The head on the block is a story scene after the fight.

Reward

An Ember Shard and the keepsake Headsman's Hood — "A black hood, worn through at the eyes. The axe in the Chronicle — “the palace headsman's axe” — was his."

Spoiler: who he is

The King ordered the ash executed so it would lie down. The ash does not lie down, and Morten has been working for a hundred years. First on his list were the ones who stole bread, hanged in the hungry winter before the feast. At the start of the second world the King sends him to "Bring me the Swallow." In detail — Morten, the One Who Brings.
